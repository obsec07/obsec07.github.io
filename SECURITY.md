# Security policy

Thanks for helping keep this blog and its readers safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately:

- **GitHub:** [open a private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) for this repository (only the owner can see it), or
- **Email:** the address on the site's [security policy page](https://obsec07.github.io/security/).

Don't post it in a reply, a public issue or on social media before it's fixed. Include the page or address, steps to
reproduce, the impact, and how to reach you. You'll usually hear back within a few days.

## Scope

- The website (https://obsec07.github.io), including the admin panel at `/admin`.
- Its replies, likes and visitor-stats server (the Cloudflare Worker in `api/`).
- The source code in this repository.

Out of scope: denial of service and load testing, spam, phishing or social engineering, physical attacks, problems
in GitHub, Cloudflare, Google Fonts or OpenStreetMap themselves, and missing headers that GitHub Pages doesn't let a
site set, self-XSS or rate limits without demonstrated impact.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy is welcome: no legal action and no complaints to your provider. Test with
your own browser, guest cookie and replies; don't access, change or delete other visitors' data beyond the minimum
needed to show the problem; don't scan at high request rates; and allow reasonable time for a fix before publishing.
There's no bug bounty, but you'll be thanked and, if you like, credited.

The same policy is on the site at https://obsec07.github.io/security/, and the contact details are machine-readable at
https://obsec07.github.io/.well-known/security.txt.
