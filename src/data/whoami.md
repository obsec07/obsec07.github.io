# Who Am I

Hey there! I'm **tobi** — an independent security researcher with a focus on web and application security. I spend most of my time hunting for bugs across authorized targets, breaking down how systems fail, and turning those findings into something others can learn from. This site is where I document that journey: the challenges I solve, the vulnerabilities I uncover, and the techniques I pick up along the way.

My work has been recognized with Hall of Fame acknowledgments from **NASA** and **Deutsche Telekom**, which reflects the kind of research I care about — real findings on real systems, reported responsibly. Beyond bug bounty work, I'm drawn to the deeper end of security: understanding root causes, chaining small issues into meaningful impact, and building the tooling that makes that process faster and more repeatable.

A big part of how I work is automation. Rather than leaning entirely on off-the-shelf scanners, I build my own reconnaissance tooling — including asynchronous JavaScript analysis and passive subdomain enumeration utilities — to keep my workflow lean, targeted, and thorough. I run a Linux-based setup tuned for speed and keyboard-driven efficiency, and I'm always refining it.

## What You'll Find Here

- **CTF Writeups** — challenge walkthroughs I found interesting or worth sharing.
- **0day / Research** — vulnerabilities and CVEs I've dug into.
- **InfoSec Blog** — notes, techniques, and lessons from the field.
- **Tools** — recon scripts and tooling I build and release.

Whether you're here to read a writeup, grab a tool, or just talk security, I'm glad you stopped by. This space is as much a personal archive as it is a place to share — and it grows every time I learn something worth writing down.
